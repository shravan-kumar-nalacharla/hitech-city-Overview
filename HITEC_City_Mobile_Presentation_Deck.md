## 📄 SLIDE 07 / 10: RISK MANAGEMENT

### 🛡️ Risk Register & Categorization (R-01 to R-10)
**Subtitle**: PMI PMBOK Academic Risk Matrix

#### 📋 Risk & Mitigation Highlights
* **R-01 (Financial Drag)**: Mitigated via PPP risk sharing & phased capex commitments.
* **R-02 (Fast-Track Quality)**: Controlled via L&T QA/QC audits & ISO safety compliance.
* **R-04 (Utility Deficits)**: Mitigated by captive 33kV substation & 100% N+1 DG backup.
* **R-05 (Telecom Shifts)**: Mitigated via STPI satellite earth station & vertical fiber risers.
* **R-06 (Market Demand Drag)**: Mitigated by securing early anchor leases (Microsoft).
* **R-09 (Policy Shifts)**: Mitigated via statutory SPV establishment & bipartisan MoUs.
